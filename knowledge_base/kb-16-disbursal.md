title: How Loan Disbursal Works
---

Disbursal is the release of the sanctioned money and happens only after the member has accepted the sanction letter and completed the loan agreement. For Personal and Business Loans the full amount is credited to the member's account in one transfer, usually within one to four working days of approval. Home Loans and Education Loans are commonly disbursed in tranches tied to construction milestones or academic semesters, and interest is charged only on the amount actually released. The first instalment falls due on a fixed date in the month following the final disbursal. A sanction that is not accepted within thirty days lapses and the application must be filed again.
