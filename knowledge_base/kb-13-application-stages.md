title: Loan Application Stages and Timelines
---

Every loan application moves through the same four stages: Submitted when the member files it, Under Review once a credit officer picks it up, then either Approved or Rejected by an underwriter, and finally Disbursed once the money is released. A credit officer normally picks up a new application within one to three days of submission. The time from submission to a decision depends heavily on the product: two to ten days for a Personal Loan, three to fourteen for an Auto Loan, seven to twenty-five for an Education Loan, ten to thirty-five for a Business Loan, and twenty to fifty days for a Home Loan, which needs a property valuation and a legal check. Disbursal follows an approval by one to four working days. An application whose review has run past three weeks is treated as stalled and is routed to a supervisor.
