title: Prepayment and Foreclosure Penalties
---

Cred charges no prepayment penalty on floating-rate Home Loans taken by individual borrowers, in line with the regulator's direction. Fixed-rate Home Loans carry a 2 percent charge on the amount prepaid if the loan is closed within the first three years. Personal Loans and Business Loans carry 3 percent of the outstanding principal if foreclosed before twelve instalments have been paid, and 2 percent after that. Part-prepayments of up to 25 percent of the outstanding principal in any financial year are free of charge on every product. No foreclosure is processed until all instalments due to date have been cleared.
