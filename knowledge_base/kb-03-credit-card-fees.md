Meridian Bank issues the Meridian Rewards Card, and the Meridian Rewards Card carries a joining fee of 499 rupees.
The Meridian Rewards Card also carries an annual fee of 999 rupees.
Meridian Bank waives the annual fee on the Meridian Rewards Card once the cardholder's annual spend crosses 2 lakh rupees.
The Meridian Rewards Card offers an interest-free period of up to 48 days on retail purchases.
A cash advance on the Meridian Rewards Card carries a cash-advance fee of 2.5 percent of the amount withdrawn, subject to a minimum of 500 rupees.
A cash advance on the Meridian Rewards Card carries no interest-free period, so interest accrues from the day of withdrawal.
The Meridian Rewards Card applies a foreign-currency markup of 3.5 percent on any transaction billed in a currency other than rupees.
Meridian Bank charges a card-replacement fee of 250 rupees for a lost or damaged Meridian Rewards Card.
Meridian Bank charges a duplicate-statement fee of 100 rupees when a cardholder requests a reprinted statement.
Goods and services tax at 18 percent applies on top of every fee levied on the Meridian Rewards Card.
Every fee on the Meridian Rewards Card appears on the statement in the billing cycle in which Meridian Bank levies it.
A cardholder can track the joining fee, the annual fee and every other Meridian Rewards Card charge through the same monthly statement.
