Meridian Bank offers five loan products, a Personal Loan, an Auto Loan, an Education Loan, a Business Loan and a Home Loan.
Meridian Bank assesses eligibility separately for each loan product, because the risk profile of an unsecured personal advance differs from that of a secured home loan.
For a salaried applicant, Meridian Bank sets the eligible age band from 21 to 60 years.
For a self-employed applicant, Meridian Bank sets the eligible age band from 21 to 65 years.
Meridian Bank requires a minimum credit score of 700 for any loan product.
A salaried applicant evidences income with salary slips and Form 16, while a self-employed applicant evidences income with audited financials and income-tax returns.
The Personal Loan at Meridian Bank runs from 50,000 rupees to 40 lakh rupees.
The Auto Loan at Meridian Bank runs from 1 lakh rupees to 25 lakh rupees.
The Education Loan at Meridian Bank runs from 1 lakh rupees to 50 lakh rupees.
The Business Loan at Meridian Bank runs from 1 lakh rupees to 50 lakh rupees.
The Home Loan at Meridian Bank runs from 10 lakh rupees to 1.5 crore rupees.
For a secured product such as the Auto Loan or the Home Loan, Meridian Bank additionally assesses the asset offered as security.
An applicant with an existing Meridian Bank relationship of 12 months or more receives a shortened assessment timeline of 3 working days.
