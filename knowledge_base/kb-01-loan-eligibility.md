title: Loan Eligibility Criteria by Loan Type
---

Cred sets a separate credit-score floor and income test for each loan product, because the risk on a secured home loan is nothing like the risk on an unsecured personal loan. A Home Loan needs a score of 750 or above and sanctions between 10 lakh and 1.5 crore; an Auto Loan needs 700 and runs from 1 lakh to 30 lakh. Personal Loans need a score of 720 and are capped at 25 lakh, while Business Loans need 700 plus at least two years of business vintage and go up to 75 lakh. An Education Loan will accept a score as low as 650 provided a co-applicant with a stable income signs on, and sanctions up to 50 lakh. Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years old at the time of application, and salaried applicants need at least six months with their current employer.
