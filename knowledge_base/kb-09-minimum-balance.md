Meridian Bank measures the minimum-balance requirement as an average monthly balance rather than a daily floor.
Meridian Bank averages this balance over the calendar month to determine whether the account met the requirement.
A metro branch account at Meridian Bank must maintain an average monthly balance of 10,000 rupees.
A semi-urban branch account at Meridian Bank must maintain an average monthly balance of 5,000 rupees.
A rural branch account at Meridian Bank must maintain an average monthly balance of 2,000 rupees.
Meridian Bank levies a shortfall charge of 150 rupees when the average monthly balance falls up to 50 percent below the requirement.
Meridian Bank levies a shortfall charge of 300 rupees when the average monthly balance falls between 50 and 75 percent below the requirement.
Meridian Bank levies a shortfall charge of 500 rupees when the average monthly balance falls more than 75 percent below the requirement.
Goods and services tax applies on top of every shortfall charge.
Meridian Bank exempts salary accounts and basic savings accounts from the minimum-balance requirement.
Meridian Bank levies the shortfall charge in the billing cycle following the month in which the shortfall occurred.
Meridian Bank notifies the customer by SMS and email before levying the shortfall charge.
