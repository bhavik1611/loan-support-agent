title: Minimum Balance Requirements
---

Cred savings accounts carry a monthly average balance requirement rather than a daily one, so a temporary dip does not trigger a charge on its own. The requirement is 10,000 rupees for accounts held at metro and urban branches, and 5,000 rupees for semi-urban and rural branches. Falling short attracts a penalty of 150 rupees a month in metro locations and 75 rupees elsewhere, debited on the first working day of the following month. Salary accounts with a credit of at least 25,000 rupees a month, and accounts held by members under 21, are exempt from the requirement entirely. The monthly average is computed across every calendar day, including days the branch is closed.
