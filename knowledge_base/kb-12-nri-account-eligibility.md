Meridian Bank treats an Indian citizen who resides outside India for employment, business or an indefinite stay as a non-resident Indian.
Meridian Bank treats a person who holds a foreign passport but has Indian ancestry as a person of Indian origin.
A non-resident may not hold a resident savings account with Meridian Bank and must convert any existing resident savings account.
Meridian Bank offers three account types to a non-resident, an NRE account, an NRO account, and an FCNR account.
Meridian Bank requires a passport, a valid visa or residence permit, overseas address proof, and a PAN for opening a non-resident account.
A non-resident can open an account with Meridian Bank from overseas by submitting attested copies of these documents.
Meridian Bank allows a resident close relative to be added to the operating mandate of a non-resident account.
Meridian Bank allows a joint NRE account to be opened between two non-residents.
Interest earned on an NRE account is exempt from Indian income tax, while interest earned on an NRO account is taxable.
A non-resident who returns to India converts the non-resident account back to a resident account with Meridian Bank.
Meridian Bank requires this conversion to resident status to happen within a reasonable period after the customer's return.
Every non-resident account with Meridian Bank remains subject to the same KYC standards Meridian Bank applies to a resident account.
