Meridian Bank finances a Home Loan against a loan-to-value ratio that steps down as the property value rises.
For a property valued up to 30 lakh rupees, Meridian Bank lends up to 90 percent of the property value.
For a property valued above 30 lakh rupees and up to 75 lakh rupees, Meridian Bank lends up to 80 percent of the property value.
For a property valued above 75 lakh rupees, Meridian Bank lends up to 75 percent of the property value.
The margin on a Meridian Home Loan is the balance the customer contributes from personal funds, and it grows as the loan-to-value slab tightens.
The Meridian Home Loan amount ranges from 10 lakh rupees to 1.5 crore rupees, and the maximum tenure on a Home Loan is 30 years.
Meridian Bank takes the financed property as security, placing a mortgage on it and verifying the title before disbursement.
Stamp duty and registration charges are excluded from the property valuation that Meridian uses to compute the loan-to-value ratio.
The Meridian Home Loan interest rate runs from 8.40 to 9.85 percent per annum.
Meridian requires an income-earning co-applicant on a Home Loan application once the property value exceeds 75 lakh rupees.
When the property financed by a Meridian Home Loan is under construction, Meridian stages the disbursement against construction milestones rather than releasing the full amount at once.
Meridian assesses Home Loan eligibility alongside the loan-to-value ratio, so both the property valuation and the applicant's income determine the final sanctioned amount.
