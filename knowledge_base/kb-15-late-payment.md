title: Late Payment Charges and Default
---

A missed loan instalment attracts penal interest of 2 percent a month on the overdue amount, charged from the due date until the arrears are cleared. Cred reports the account to the credit bureaus once an instalment is more than thirty days overdue, and that report stays on the member's record for thirty-six months. An account ninety days past due is classified as non-performing, at which point the full outstanding becomes payable and recovery action begins. Members in genuine difficulty can request a restructure before the ninety-day mark, which may extend the tenure and lower the instalment. A restructure is itself reported to the bureaus and will lower the score, but far less than a default.
