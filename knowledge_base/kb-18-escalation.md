title: Escalation and Grievance Redressal
---

A member unhappy with an outcome should first raise it with support, which holds a complaint reference and a commitment to respond within seven working days. If the response is unsatisfactory or does not arrive, the complaint escalates to the nodal officer, who must resolve it within thirty days of the original complaint date. Cases involving a suspected fraud flag, or an application whose review has stalled well beyond its product's usual window, are escalated by Cred without waiting for the member to complain. If thirty days pass without a resolution, the member may approach the RBI Ombudsman directly and free of charge. Every escalation keeps its original complaint reference so the history travels with the case.
