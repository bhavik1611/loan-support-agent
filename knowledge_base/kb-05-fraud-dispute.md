A Meridian Bank customer can report an unauthorised transaction through the 24-hour helpline, net banking, the mobile app, or any branch.
Reporting an unauthorised transaction to Meridian Bank within 3 working days preserves zero liability for the customer.
Reporting an unauthorised transaction between 4 and 7 working days carries limited liability capped at 25,000 rupees.
Reporting an unauthorised transaction beyond 7 working days leaves Meridian Bank to decide liability case by case.
Meridian Bank blocks the affected card or channel immediately once a customer reports an unauthorised transaction.
Meridian Bank acknowledges the report with a reference number within 24 hours.
Meridian Bank credits the disputed amount provisionally within 10 working days of the report.
Meridian Bank completes its investigation into a fraud dispute within 90 days.
Meridian Bank asks the customer to sign a written declaration describing the unauthorised transaction.
If the investigation finds the transaction was in fact authorised, Meridian Bank reverses the provisional credit after giving 15 days notice.
A customer dissatisfied with how Meridian Bank has handled a fraud dispute can escalate the matter to the banking ombudsman 30 days after an unsatisfactory response.
Meridian Bank tracks every fraud dispute against the reference number issued at acknowledgement until the investigation closes.
