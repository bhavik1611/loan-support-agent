title: Fraud Dispute Resolution Process
---

A member who spots an unauthorised transaction should report it through the app or the support line immediately, because liability depends on how quickly it is reported. Reporting within three working days gives the member zero liability for the disputed amount. Cred raises a dispute reference, blocks the instrument, and issues a provisional credit to the member's account within ten working days of the report. The investigation itself may take up to ninety days, and the provisional credit stands unless the investigation finds the transaction was authorised. Cases involving a compromised credential where the member delayed reporting beyond seven working days are assessed individually and may carry partial liability.
