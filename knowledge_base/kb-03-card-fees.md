title: Credit Card Fee Structure
---

Cred credit cards carry a joining fee between 500 and 5,000 rupees depending on the variant, and the same amount again as an annual fee from the second year. The annual fee is waived in full if the cardholder spends 2 lakh rupees or more in the preceding card year. Late payment charges are tiered by the outstanding balance: nothing below 500 rupees, 500 rupees between 500 and 10,000, and 1,300 rupees above 50,000. Cash advances attract 2.5 percent of the withdrawn amount subject to a 500 rupee minimum, and interest runs from the withdrawal date with no grace period. Foreign currency transactions carry a markup of 3.5 percent on the converted rupee value.
