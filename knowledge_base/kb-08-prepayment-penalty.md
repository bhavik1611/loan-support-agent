Meridian Bank charges no prepayment penalty on a floating-rate loan taken by an individual borrower.
Meridian Bank charges a prepayment penalty of 2 percent of the amount prepaid on a fixed-rate loan.
Meridian Bank charges a prepayment penalty of 3 percent of the amount prepaid on a Business Loan, regardless of whether the Business Loan carries a fixed or a floating rate.
Meridian Bank imposes a lock-in period of 6 months or 6 paid instalments, whichever is later, before allowing any prepayment.
Goods and services tax at 18 percent applies on top of the prepayment penalty charge.
A borrower must give Meridian Bank 15 days written notice before making a prepayment.
Meridian Bank applies the lower end of the prepayment schedule when the borrower prepays from their own funds.
Meridian Bank applies the full prepayment charge when the borrower is refinancing the loan through another lender.
Meridian Bank computes the prepayment charge on the outstanding principal at the time of prepayment, not on the original sanctioned amount.
A borrower planning a large prepayment should confirm the applicable lock-in period with Meridian Bank before submitting the request.
The prepayment penalty on a Meridian Bank loan is calculated afresh for each prepayment made during the tenure.
Meridian Bank does not waive the prepayment penalty on a Business Loan even where the borrower prepays from own funds.
