title: Account Closure Process
---

A member may close a savings account by submitting a closure request through the app or at any branch, along with any unused cheque leaves and the debit card. Accounts closed after twelve months of opening carry no closure charge; those closed within twelve months attract a 500 rupee fee. All standing instructions, auto-debits and linked recurring payments must be cancelled by the member before the request is processed, since Cred will not unwind them on the member's behalf. The residual balance is transferred to a nominated account and the closure completes within fifteen working days. An account carrying an active loan, a lien, or an unresolved dispute cannot be closed until that matter is settled.
