Meridian Bank sets a detailed eligibility bar for its Personal Loan that goes beyond the general criteria applied across products.
A salaried applicant for a Meridian Personal Loan needs a minimum net monthly income of 25,000 rupees.
A self-employed applicant for the same Personal Loan needs a minimum annual profit after tax of 3 lakh rupees.
Meridian also looks at work stability, requiring at least 2 years of total work experience with 1 year in the current job for a salaried applicant, or 3 years in the current business for a self-employed applicant.
The Personal Loan amount at Meridian Bank ranges from 50,000 rupees to 40 lakh rupees, and the tenure runs from 12 to 60 months.
A credit score check applies to every Personal Loan applicant, and the income evidence Meridian collects mirrors the general documentation used for other loan products at the bank.
Meridian caps the fixed-obligation-to-income ratio for a Personal Loan applicant at 55 percent, a ratio specific to this product and not part of the bank's general eligibility screen.
The Personal Loan is unsecured, so Meridian takes no collateral against the sanctioned amount.
Meridian charges a processing fee of 2 percent of the sanctioned Personal Loan amount, subject to a minimum of 2,500 rupees.
Adding a co-applicant to a Meridian Personal Loan application can lift the eligible loan amount by up to 40 percent.
Meridian asks a Personal Loan applicant for salary slips or business proof specific to the applicant's employment category, in addition to the standard identity and address documents.
The amount Meridian sanctions for a Personal Loan can come in lower than the amount the applicant originally applied for, based on the fixed-obligation-to-income ratio and the credit assessment.
