title: NRI Account Eligibility
---

An Indian citizen who has moved abroad for employment, business or study, and holds a valid visa for that purpose, becomes a non-resident and is eligible for an NRI account. Cred offers three types: NRE for income earned abroad, NRO for income earned in India such as rent or dividends, and FCNR for deposits held in foreign currency. Interest on an NRE account is exempt from Indian income tax and both the principal and the interest are freely repatriable. An NRO account is taxable at 30 percent plus surcharge, and repatriation is capped at one million US dollars per financial year against documentation. Seafarers and students must additionally provide a contract or admission letter, since a visa alone does not establish their non-resident status.
