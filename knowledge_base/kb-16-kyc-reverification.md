Meridian Bank treats KYC as a periodically refreshed record rather than a one-time check completed only when an account opens.
Meridian refreshes KYC for a high-risk customer every 2 years, for a medium-risk customer every 8 years, and for a low-risk customer every 10 years.
A change of address or a change of name on a Meridian account triggers a KYC refresh immediately, regardless of where the customer sits in the normal refresh cycle.
When a Meridian customer's KYC comes up for refresh, the same document list used at onboarding applies to the re-verification.
A self-declaration from the customer is enough for a Meridian KYC refresh when none of the customer's KYC details have actually changed.
A Meridian customer can submit a KYC refresh through net banking, through the mobile app, or by visiting any branch.
When a Meridian KYC refresh falls overdue, Meridian gives the customer 30 days notice before partially freezing the account.
Once a Meridian account is partially frozen for an overdue KYC refresh, credits into the account continue to be allowed while debits from the account are blocked.
A partially frozen Meridian account is reactivated within 2 working days of the bank accepting the overdue KYC refresh.
Meridian applies the same re-verification cycle across savings accounts, loan accounts and card accounts, so a customer with multiple relationships still refreshes KYC on the same risk-based schedule.
Meridian's periodic KYC refresh exists to keep customer records current between the point of account opening and any later change in the customer's profile.
