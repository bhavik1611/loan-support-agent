title: Documents Required for a Home Loan
---

A Home Loan needs everything the standard KYC pack requires plus a full set of property papers, which is why it takes far longer to process than an unsecured loan. The property documents are the sale agreement, the chain of title deeds, the approved building plan, and an encumbrance certificate covering the last thirteen years. Cred commissions its own valuation and legal opinion on the property and does not accept reports arranged by the applicant or the builder. Salaried applicants add Form 16 for the last two years alongside their payslips, and self-employed applicants add audited financial statements. A missing or disputed title document is the single most common reason a Home Loan sits at Under Review for longer than its usual window.
