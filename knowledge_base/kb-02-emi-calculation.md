title: How EMI Is Calculated
---

An equated monthly instalment is a fixed monthly payment that covers both interest and principal, worked out so the loan closes exactly at the end of its tenure. Cred uses the standard reducing-balance formula, where the instalment equals P times r times (1+r) raised to n, divided by (1+r) raised to n minus one, with P the sanctioned amount, r the monthly interest rate and n the number of months. Early instalments are mostly interest and later ones mostly principal, which is why prepaying in the first third of the tenure saves far more than prepaying near the end. A 30 lakh Home Loan at 8.5 percent over 240 months carries an EMI of roughly 26,035 rupees. Any part-prepayment shortens the tenure by default rather than reducing the instalment, unless the borrower asks for the opposite in writing.
