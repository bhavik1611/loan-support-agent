Meridian Bank reads a credit score on a range from 300 to 900.
Meridian Bank treats a credit score of 750 and above as good.
Meridian Bank sets a minimum credit score of 700 to qualify for a loan.
Repayment history is the largest single factor in the credit score Meridian Bank reads, carrying a weight of 35 percent.
Credit utilisation carries a weight of 30 percent, and Meridian Bank recommends keeping credit utilisation below 30 percent of the available limit.
The age of the credit history carries a weight of 15 percent.
Credit mix, meaning the variety of loan and card products held, carries a weight of 10 percent.
New credit enquiries carry a weight of 10 percent, and each hard enquiry costs the applicant 5 to 10 points.
Several hard enquiries within a short window compound, so the point loss from multiple enquiries is larger than the point loss from one enquiry.
A settled account, meaning a loan closed for less than the full amount owed, marks the credit report for seven years.
Every entry on the credit report, whether positive or negative, stays on the report for seven years.
Checking one's own credit score counts as a soft enquiry, and a soft enquiry costs the applicant nothing.
