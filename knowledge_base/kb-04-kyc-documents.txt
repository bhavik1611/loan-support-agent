Meridian Bank requires completed KYC verification before opening any account or disbursing any loan.
Meridian Bank accepts a passport, a voter identity card, a driving licence, an Aadhaar card, or a job card issued under NREGA as proof of identity.
Meridian Bank accepts a passport, a utility bill no older than two months, a property tax receipt, or a bank statement as proof of address.
Meridian Bank makes PAN mandatory for any single transaction of 50,000 rupees or above.
An applicant can complete Aadhaar-based verification with Meridian Bank entirely online.
A self-employed applicant additionally submits business registration proof and the last two years of income-tax returns to Meridian Bank.
A non-resident applicant additionally submits a passport, a valid visa, and overseas address proof to Meridian Bank.
Meridian Bank requires original documents for in-person verification, or self-attested and notarised copies where the applicant cannot appear in person.
An incomplete document file freezes a Meridian Bank application rather than causing an outright rejection.
Meridian Bank resumes processing a frozen application as soon as the applicant submits the missing document.
Every proof of identity and proof of address that Meridian Bank accepts must be current and legible at the time of submission.
Meridian Bank applies the same KYC document list to both an account opening and a loan application.
