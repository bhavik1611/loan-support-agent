title: KYC Document Requirements
---

Every applicant must complete Know Your Customer verification before an account is opened or a loan is disbursed. A PAN card is mandatory for identity and is the only document Cred will not substitute. Address may be proved with an Aadhaar letter, passport, voter identity card or driving licence, and the address on it must match the one on the application. Income proof depends on employment type: salaried applicants submit three months of payslips and six months of bank statements, while self-employed applicants submit two years of filed income tax returns. Re-verification falls due every eight years for low-risk customers and every two years for those classified high risk.
