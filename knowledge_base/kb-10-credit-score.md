title: What Moves a Credit Score
---

A credit score summarises repayment behaviour and is rebuilt each month from the data lenders report to the bureaus. Payment history is the largest single input at roughly 35 percent, so a single instalment more than 30 days late does more damage than any other event. Credit utilisation, meaning the share of available card limit actually used, accounts for around 30 percent, and staying under 30 percent utilisation is the fastest lever most members have. The age of the oldest account contributes about 15 percent, which is why closing an old card can lower a score rather than raise it. The remaining weight splits between the mix of secured and unsecured borrowing and the number of hard enquiries in the last twelve months.
