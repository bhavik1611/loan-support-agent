title: Joint Account Rules
---

A Cred savings account may be held jointly by up to four holders, and the operating mandate is fixed when the account is opened. Either or Survivor allows any single holder to operate the account and passes it to the survivor on a death; Former or Survivor restricts operation to the first-named holder while they are alive. Jointly requires every holder to sign each instruction, which is safer but slower for day-to-day use. Changing the mandate later needs the written consent of every living holder, and adding a new holder is treated as a fresh account opening with full KYC. Each holder is individually liable for the full overdrawn balance regardless of who caused it.
