Meridian Bank operates the NRE account and the NRO account under different rules for what money can enter each account.
A Meridian NRE account accepts only foreign-earned income that is remitted into it from overseas.
A Meridian NRO account accepts Indian-sourced income such as rent, dividends and pension credited from within India.
Funds held in a Meridian NRE account are freely repatriable outside India without any annual limit.
Funds held in a Meridian NRO account can be repatriated only up to the equivalent of 1 million US dollars in a financial year, and that repatriation needs Forms 15CA and 15CB.
Interest earned on a Meridian NRE account is exempt from Indian income tax.
Interest earned on a Meridian NRO account is taxed at source at 30 percent plus surcharge and cess.
Meridian allows an NRO account to be held jointly with a resident, but a Meridian NRE account cannot be held jointly with a resident.
Meridian permits funds to move freely from an NRE account into an NRO account, while a transfer from an NRO account into an NRE account is allowed only within the repatriation cap and only with the same repatriation forms.
Both the NRE account and the NRO account at Meridian Bank can be opened as a savings account or as a term deposit.
A rupee cheque issued by a resident and deposited at Meridian Bank can be credited only to the NRO account, never to the NRE account.
Repatriating funds out of a Meridian NRO account requires the same Forms 15CA and 15CB regardless of the amount being moved within the annual cap.
