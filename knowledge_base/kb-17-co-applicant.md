title: Co-Applicants and Guarantors
---

A co-applicant shares both the ownership of the asset and the liability for the loan, while a guarantor carries the liability without any claim on the asset. Cred requires a co-applicant on every Education Loan, and accepts one voluntarily on other products where the primary applicant's income or score falls short. The co-applicant's income is added to the primary applicant's when the eligible amount is computed, which is the usual reason members add one. Both parties complete full KYC, and the loan appears on the credit report of both, so a default damages both scores equally. A co-applicant cannot be removed during the tenure; doing so requires the loan to be refinanced afresh.
