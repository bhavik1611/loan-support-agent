title: Interest Rate Slabs
---

Interest rates at Cred are banded by product and then adjusted within the band by the applicant's credit score. Home Loans run from 8.25 to 10.5 percent, and Auto Loans from 8.75 to 13 percent, both on a floating basis linked to the external benchmark. Education Loans run from 8.5 to 12.5 percent, Personal Loans from 10.5 to 18 percent, and Business Loans from 11 to 19 percent. An applicant scoring above 780 receives the floor of their product band, while one at the eligibility minimum receives the ceiling. Floating rates reset quarterly when the benchmark moves, and the borrower is notified of the revised instalment before it takes effect.
